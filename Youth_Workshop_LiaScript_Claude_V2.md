### What IS Artificial Intelligence?

                    --{{0}}--
Now let's define what we mean by Artificial Intelligence. Think of it this way...

**Simple Definition:**

> **Artificial Intelligence** is the ability of computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence – like understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions, and learning from experience.

#### Key Terms to Know

**Algorithm**
: A set of instructions or rules a computer follows to solve a problem (like a recipe for cooking!)

**Machine Learning** 
: An approach where algorithms learn from data instead of being explicitly programmed. Just like you learn from experience!

**Neural Network**
: A type of AI model inspired by the human brain, made of interconnected "nodes" that can learn patterns

**Generative AI**
: AI that can create new content like text, images, music, or videos based on learned patterns

                    --{{1}}--
The real breakthrough in recent years has been generative AI. Tools like ChatGPT for text and DALL-E for images can actually create new content that never existed before, using patterns they learned from massive amounts of data.

              {{1}}
> **🚀 Recent Breakthrough:** Generative AI tools like **ChatGPT** (text) and **DALL-E** (images) can create entirely new content, not just analyze existing data!

### Optional Learning: AI Ethics & Limitations

                    --{{0}}--
Before we dive into creating, let's briefly explore some important considerations about AI.

**Click to explore key concepts:**

                      {{1}}
??[AI Bias](https://www.youtube.com/embed/59bMh59JQDo "Understanding AI Bias - 3 minute explainer")

                      {{2}}
**AI "Hallucinations"**

AI sometimes generates false information that sounds convincing. For example:
- ChatGPT might confidently give wrong historical dates
- Image generators might create people with extra fingers
- AI might cite fake research papers that don't exist

> **Why this happens:** AI predicts likely next words/pixels based on training data, but doesn't verify facts.

                      {{3}}
**The Data Question**

All AI systems learn from data created by humans, which means:
- If training data has biases, AI inherits them
- If certain groups are underrepresented, AI might not work well for them
- AI reflects patterns in human-created content, including problematic ones

                      {{4}}
**Responsible AI Use**

> **Key Principles:**
> - Always verify important information from AI
> - Be aware AI might reflect biases
> - Use AI as a tool to enhance human creativity, not replace human judgment
> - Consider the source and quality of AI training data

### Main Project: "AI for the Future"

> **Time:** 25 minutes  
> **Challenge:** Design an AI solution for tomorrow's workplace

                    --{{0}}--
Now for the main creative challenge! Your team will imagine and design a new AI tool that could positively impact the future workplace or society.

#### Project Brief

**Your Mission:** Create a new AI-powered invention that could solve a problem or improve work in some field.

**Deliverable:** A mini-poster or 1-minute pitch presentation

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Ideate (5 minutes)**
Brainstorm problems in areas like:
- Healthcare & medicine
- Education & learning  
- Environment & sustainability
- Entertainment & creativity
- Daily life & productivity
- Agriculture & food
- Transportation & logistics

Pick one idea that excites your team!

**Step 2: AI-Assisted Content Creation (7-8 minutes)**
Use the generative tools to create:
- **Image:** Generate a picture/logo representing your AI idea
- **Text:** Ask the chatbot to help write a catchy tagline or benefits list

Remember: You're the creative directors - use AI to enhance your ideas, not replace them!

**Step 3: Build Your Presentation (5 minutes)**
Compile your materials into:
- AI tool **name & purpose**
- **Image** (generated or drawn)
- **2-3 key benefits**
- **One line about why this is beneficial**

**Step 4: Prepare to Present (2-3 minutes)**
Decide who will speak and practice your 1-minute pitch!

## Facilitator Notes

                    --{{0}}--
This section contains important implementation notes for workshop facilitators.

#### Technical Preparation

**Before the Workshop:**
- Test all AI tools on the school network
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Prepare backup access methods (pre-made accounts, alternative tools)
- Have screenshots ready in case of technical difficulties
- Verify browser compatibility

**Device Requirements:**
- One internet-enabled device per team (4-6 students)
- Projector/screen for demonstrations
- Backup devices if possible

#### Time Management Tips

**Critical Timing:**
- **Discover Phase:** Don't let discussions run too long
- **Create Phase:** Give 2-minute warnings before transitions  
- **Share Phase:** Strictly enforce 1-minute presentation limit
- Use visible countdown timer for activities

**Flexibility Options:**
- **Smaller groups:** Reduce exploration tools or use pair-sharing instead of full presentations
- **Time constraints:** Skip optional learning sections or combine team discussions
- **Technical issues:** Have drawing materials as backup for image generation

#### Engagement Strategies

**For Mixed English Proficiency:**
- Speak clearly and check for understanding
- Use visual aids and demonstrations
- Encourage peer translation within teams
- Welcome questions and create safe space for clarification

**For Diverse Tech Comfort:**
- Pair confident users with less experienced students
- Rotate keyboard control within teams
- Focus on ideas over technical execution
- Celebrate all contributions equally

#### Safety & Ethics

**Content Monitoring:**
- Circulate during hands-on activities
- Quickly address any inappropriate AI outputs
- Emphasize school-appropriate use guidelines
- Have clear escalation procedures

**Inclusive Environment:**
- Ensure all voices are heard in team activities
- Acknowledge different perspectives on AI
- Validate concerns about technology and employment
- Promote collaborative rather than competitive atmosphere
