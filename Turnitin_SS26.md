# What is Turnitin?

<div style="background:#E3F2FD; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #1E88E5; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## Turnitin Overview

Turnitin® is a digital tool used by **students and instructors** to identify potential instances of **plagiarism in academic work**.

It compares submitted papers with:

- Academic publications  
- Internet sources  
- Previously submitted student papers  

</div>

<div style="background:#E8F5E9; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #43A047; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## Similarity Report

After submitting your assignment to Turnitin®, the system generates a **Similarity Report**.

This report highlights text that is similar to other sources and helps you:

- Review your writing  
- Identify possible citation issues  
- Improve your academic work  

</div>

<div style="background:#FFF8E1; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #F9A825; border-radius:8px;">

## Important for Students

Students should carefully review the similarity report and make **necessary revisions**, such as:

- Correcting citations  
- Paraphrasing properly  
- Adding references where needed  

This helps ensure **academic integrity** in your work.

</div>

> **Source:** Writing Center UAGC (2025)
# What Does Turnitin Do?

<div style="background:#E3F2FD; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #1E88E5; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## Similarity Detection

Turnitin® highlights **sentences or phrases that are similar to other sources**, such as:

- Previously submitted papers  
- Published articles  
- Websites and online materials  

These similarities appear in the **Turnitin Similarity Report**.

</div>

<div style="background:#E8F5E9; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #43A047; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## Similarity ≠ Plagiarism

Similarity is **not always plagiarism**.

For example:

- A **direct quotation**  
- A **correctly cited reference**  
- Commonly used academic phrases  

These may appear similar but are **acceptable when cited correctly**.

</div>

<div style="background:#FFF8E1; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #F9A825; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## Correct Academic Practice

If you use information from a source, you should:

- **Summarize** the idea in your own words  
- **Paraphrase** the content properly  
- **Use quotation marks** for direct quotes  
- **Cite the source correctly**

</div>

<div style="background:#FFEBEE; padding:18px; border-left:6px solid #E53935; border-radius:8px; margin-bottom:18px;">

## When Does It Become Plagiarism?

It becomes plagiarism when:

- Text is copied **without citation**  
- Ideas are used **without referencing the original source**  
- Material is not properly **paraphrased or quoted**

Always cite outside sources to avoid plagiarism.

</div>

> **Source:** Writing Center UAGC (2025)

# How do I Upload Assignments or Presentations on Turnitin? 
 ## Guidelines for uploading assignments on Turnitin
  ## Accessing Turnitin
 You can log in to Turnitin using the following link: Turnitin Login.

 ## Enrolling in Your Class
After logging in, you will need to enroll in your designated class by entering the Class ID and the corresponding Enrollment Key.Note: Class IDs and Enrollment Keys are updated each semester, so ensure you have the correct information for the current semester.

 ## Uploading Assignments and Presentations
All assignments and presentations for your respective courses must be uploaded to Turnitin before submitting them to your professors. This ensures your work is properly checked for originality.

# Guidelines for uploading assignments on Turnitin 

 ## Submission Deadline
Once the submission deadline has passed, the submission portal will automatically close. Please make sure to upload your work on time.

 ## Upload Limitations
You are allowed a maximum of two uploads per assignment. Please review your file carefully before submitting, as additional uploads will not be accepted after the second one.

 ## Turnitin Similarity Report
After uploading, Turnitin will generate a similarity report. You can view this report to assess the originality of your work.Please review the report carefully and make any necessary revisions to your document before submitting again.
# Guidelines for uploading assignments on Turnitin 
 ## No Deletions
Once an assignment is uploaded to Turnitin, it cannot be deleted. Please ensure that the file you upload is final before submitting it.

 ## File Format Requirements
Turnitin supports common file formats like Word .docx, and Power points .pptx. Ensure your assignment is uploaded in one of these formats.

 ## Group Submissions
If your assignment or presentation is a group project, only one group member should upload the final presentation or assignment on behalf of all members. Uploading multiple versions may be flagged as plagiarism.Make sure to include the names of all group members in the uploaded document to ensure proper grading.
