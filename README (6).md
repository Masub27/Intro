# Part 1 — Our Perception of TVET







## Why TVET Matters

 ## Key Reasons TVET is Essential

- 🏭 **Workforce Readiness** — Produces skilled workers industry needs immediately
- 📉 **Reduces Unemployment** — Targets youth and adults seeking employment
- 💡 **Promotes Innovation** — Technical skills drive productivity and innovation
- 🌍 **Supports Inclusive Growth** — Accessible to disadvantaged groups and regions

---

# TVET & Economic Development

 ## Economic Impact

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Skilled Labor Supply | Increases national productivity |
| Industry Competitiveness | Boosts exports and GDP growth |
| Entrepreneurship | Encourages small business creation |
| Foreign Investment | Attracts companies needing skilled workers |

> Skilled workers = stronger economies. TVET creates that workforce.

---

# TVET & Social Development

 ## Social Dimensions of TVET

- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Social Mobility** — Enables people from low-income backgrounds to move up
- ♀️ **Gender Equity** — Opens technical careers to women
- 🧑‍🦽 **Inclusion** — Serves persons with disabilities and marginalized youth
- 🏘️ **Community Development** — Strengthens local economies and livelihoods
- 🕊️ **Reduces Inequality** — Narrows skill and income gaps

---

# Our Group's Perception

 ## TVET as the Key Factor

We believe TVET is **the most direct policy lever** for simultaneous economic and social development because:

1. It is **demand-driven** — aligned with real market needs
2. It is **faster to deploy** than university education
3. It creates **immediate employment impact**
4. It **empowers individuals** while serving national development goals

> TVET turns human potential into national prosperity.

# Part 3 — TVET Sustainable Development Strategy

 ## In the Context of Company Development

A company-level TVET strategy ensures that **workforce development aligns with business goals** while contributing to **national sustainable development**.

> **Vision:** Build a skilled, adaptable, and socially responsible workforce that drives company growth and national progress.

---

# Strategy Framework

 ## 4 Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Focus Area |
|--------|------------|
| 🎯 **Alignment** | Match TVET programs to company skill needs |
| 🤝 **Partnership** | Collaborate with TVET institutions & government |
| ♻️ **Sustainability** | Embed ESG principles into training programs |
| 📊 **Measurement** | Track outcomes: employment, productivity, equity |

---

# Strategy — Pillar 1 & 2

 ## Alignment & Partnership

**Alignment with Business Needs:**
- Conduct annual skills gap analysis per department
- Co-design curricula with TVET schools
- Create internal apprenticeship programs

**Partnership Actions:**
- Sign MoUs with technical colleges
- Offer company sites as training labs
- Sponsor scholarships for TVET students

---

# Strategy — Pillar 3 & 4

 ## Sustainability & Measurement

**Sustainability Integration:**
- Train workers on green technologies
- Integrate SDG awareness into all TVET programs
- Promote circular economy skills (waste management, energy efficiency)

**Measurement & Evaluation:**
- Track % of graduates employed within 6 months
- Measure productivity gains post-training
- Report annually on social impact and equity metrics

---

# Summary & Conclusion

 ## Key Takeaways

> **1.** TVET is a proven **key driver** of economic and social development — it produces workers, reduces poverty, and empowers communities.

> **2.** TVET implementation must be **multi-channel**: formal schools, workplace learning, and digital platforms working together.

> **3.** Companies play a **critical role** by co-investing in TVET, aligning it to business needs, and measuring its sustainable impact.
